Practice
Theory of Ship is a naval architecture consultancy working with shipyards, owners and marine engineers in the Gulf and beyond.
Experience

Stability & strength

Sea trials
Trial agendas, bollard pull, speed runs, noise and vibration measurement, and commissioning documentation. Attendance across the UAE and the Gulf.
Basic engineering
Hull form and hydrostatics, weight estimate and control, resistance and powering, general arrangement, scantling calculation and class submission.
Class
